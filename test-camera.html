📹 Camera Access Test

This page tests if your browser can access the camera for QR scanning.

⚠️ Important: Camera access requires HTTPS in most browsers. If you're on HTTP (http://), the camera won't work.
Checking browser support...

Troubleshooting:

  • Using HTTP? Switch to HTTPS (see ENABLE_HTTPS_GUIDE.md)
  • Permission denied? Check browser settings → Camera permissions
  • No camera found? Ensure camera is connected and not used by another app
  • Still not working? Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
